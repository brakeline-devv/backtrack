03

Most strategies don't pass the gate

A strategy moves draft → backtested → paper → live. It only reaches paper if it beats simply holding, risk for risk. Only you can approve live.

ParsePlain words into rules you can read in strategy.md and risk.md.
Backtest~3 years of daily prices, next-open fills, slippage, gap-aware stops.
The gate10+ trades, drawdown under your limit, profit, and better return per unit of risk than holding.
PaperDaily entry signals, sized to your Brakeline caps. Fills recorded per strategy.
Check exitsTake profit, stop loss, max hold and RSI exits on every open position.
Live: human onlyThe agent has no tool to go live. You approve it, and Brakeline guards every order.

05

A backtest should be
allowed to say no

////////////////////////// Our rule //////////////////////////

Strategy tools usually flatter you: perfect fills, no slippage, results measured against nothing.

Backtrack fills every signal at the next open, assumes the stop hits before the target, and puts every result next to simply holding the same stocks.

Most ideas don't survive that. That is the point: you find out on paper, not with your savings.

And when one does survive, it still has to earn its way through paper trading. Live is your decision, never the agent's.
